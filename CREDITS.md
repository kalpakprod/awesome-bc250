## Photos

Hardware and build photos in the docs are **community-contributed**, credited to the member who posted them (name + a link to the source message). They are mirrored here for the handbook's educational value. If a photo — or a credit — is yours and you want it changed or removed, open an issue titled `credit: <file>` and it will be handled promptly.
