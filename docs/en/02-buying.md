# Buying Guide

> **TL;DR** — There's no official retail channel; everyone buys second-hand. The two main routes are **eBay via a "байер" (buyer/forwarder service)** if you're outside the US, and **domestic marketplaces** — **Ozon** and **Avito** in Russia, **OLX** in Ukraine, **AliExpress** worldwide, plus local hand-to-hand. As of 2025–2026 a bare board runs roughly **$70–130 / 13k–25k ₽** depending on what's bundled (PSU, SSD, cooler) and how hot the market is. Prices are **demand-driven and volatile** — they spiked from ~$45 to ~$125 in early 2025 — so treat any single price as a snapshot, check several listings, and watch for pump-and-dump and dead ("отвальная") boards.

The BC-250 was never sold at retail to consumers — it's decommissioned mining/server hardware, so **everything is second-hand or grey-market**. Where you buy depends mostly on your country and whether you're willing to wait a month for an international forward.

## Route 1 — eBay through a forwarder ("байер" / "посредник")

If you're not in the US, the established method is to buy the eBay listing through a forwarding service. The community wrote this up step by step ([src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/41598)):

1. Go to eBay, search **`bc-250`**, pick an offer that fits your budget.
2. Come back to the chat and search the topic for the keywords **"байер"** and **"посредник"** — over the chat's lifetime many forwarder contacts have been shared; check each and pick one that fits.
3. DM your chosen forwarder the eBay link; ask them to quote the **total in rubles at their rate including their commission**, plus rough shipping to your city.
4. Sum it up, pay to their card, and **wait ~a month** — or walk away.

Typical forwarder economics reported in the chat: commission around **7%**, and shipping to KZ/Russia adding roughly **1–2k ₽** on top of the eBay price. People have used buyers from **Chelyabinsk** and **Kazakhstan** with good results. ([7% / Chelyabinsk src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/21260), [KZ buyer src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/20434))

One member's all-in cost through a forwarder landed at **$630** for a built unit; another quoted **~$69 board → ~$94 in four days** as the listing's price climbed (see prices below). ([$630 src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/38483), [climb src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/36286))

## What you actually get

Listings vary wildly in what's included. Match the price to the bundle:

- **Board only** — the cheapest tier. You still need a [PSU](03-power-supply.md), [cooling](04-cooling.md), storage, and a [case](05-case.md) before it's usable. Bare-board prices below.
- **Board + PSU** — most often a **LOP300** (a ~300 W server brick the community settled on as the simplest way to feed it) or a Flex/ITX PSU. ([LOP300 bundle src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/50397), [src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/90549))
- **Board + PSU + SSD** — a "starter" set; common on Avito/Ozon.
- **Full build** — assembled, BIOS modded, OS installed, cooled, sometimes cased — the 20k–25k ₽ Барахолка units above.

There is **no "official bundle"** — heatsink presence, PSU model, and SSD size all differ listing to listing, so read the description carefully.

## Prices & what's a good deal

Prices are **set by demand, not by a manufacturer**, and they move fast. Treat these as **dated snapshots, not current truth** — always check several live listings before buying.

**eBay price climb, early 2025** (one popular lot, USD): the same listing went **$45–65 → $69 (Sat) → $79 (Mon) → $89 → $94** over about a week as ~70 units sold; the chat watched it rise to **$100–125**. The community consensus: *it goes up because people keep buying, and only drops when they stop.* ([climb src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/36286), [$100–125 src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/36273))

**Late 2025:** a member reported **"+3k ₽ to the price in a week"** during a demand spike — confirming the volatility hadn't settled. ([src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/105565))

## Scams & risks to avoid

- **Pump-and-dump / hype pricing.** The chat explicitly flagged the price action as demand-driven hype and even linked the Wikipedia "pump and dump" article. Don't FOMO-buy at a spike; prices ebb when buying slows. ([context src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/36286))
- **Dead / "отвальная" boards sold as working.** "Отвальная", "под ремонт", or "не подаёт сигнал" mean the APU is likely damaged (often after a botched cooling mod). Fine as a cheap parts donor, **not** as your first working board. ([example src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/102794), [repair listing](https://www.avito.ru/polevskoy/tovary_dlya_kompyutera/asrock_amd_bc250_pod_remont_8094775374))
- **Overpriced relistings.** Community reaction to a marked-up listing was simply *"the price is meh"* — cross-check against the ranges above before paying. ([src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/140181))
- **Commercial sellers in the Барахолка.** The topic bans commerce; a private "от человека к человеку" sale with the **@B1zon_RTX guarantor** is safer than an anonymous shop. ([rule src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/89793))
- **Forwarder risk.** You're paying a stranger and waiting a month. Use forwarders the chat has **vouched for** (e.g. the 7%-commission Chelyabinsk buyer), not a random contact. ([src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/21260))
- **Verify it's a real BC-250**, especially on AliExpress/Avito where similar AMD APU/4700S boards and other GPUs get cross-listed. Check the photos against [01-what-is-bc250.md](01-what-is-bc250.md).

## Group buys ("групповой заказ")

Because eBay pricing is so listing-and-volume sensitive, members coordinate purchases — sharing live listings, forwarder contacts, and timing — so buyers move on the same low-priced lot together and split forwarding logistics. The Барахолка's "pair of boards" sales and the practice of pooling around a single forwarder are the everyday form this takes. ([pair src](https://t.me/c/2424231195/90549), [forwarder workflow](https://t.me/c/2424231195/41598)) If you're new, **ask in the chat before buying** — someone is often already mid-order and can fold you in or steer you off a bad seller.
